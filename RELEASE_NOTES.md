# Aperture 0.9.1

A small bug fix update.

## Downloads
- **Windows 10/11 (x64):** `Aperture-0.9.1-windows-x64-setup.exe`
- **macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon):** `Aperture-0.9.1-macos-arm64.dmg`
- **Linux (x86-64):** `Aperture-0.9.1-x86_64.AppImage`, or `Aperture-0.9.1-linux-x64.tar.xz`

Already on 0.9.0? Open **Settings → About → Check for updates**.

## Fixed
- **Sound now follows your audio output.** When you plugged in headphones, connected or disconnected Bluetooth earbuds, or switched the output in your system's sound settings, Aperture kept playing through the device it started with, even after that device was gone. Until now, a restart was the only fix. Playback now moves to the new output straight away, with audio boost on or off.
